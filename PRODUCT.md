# Swipe Republic

## Register

product

## Users

People playing short browser sessions on phones or desktops. They describe a society, read a political demand, and choose between two costly actions. The game must remain readable and playable with touch, mouse, or keyboard.

## Product Purpose

A Reigns-style game with player-defined settings, recurring characters, four competing factions, and consequences that survive succession. Engagement comes from difficult choices, promises returning, and the struggle to leave office on one's own terms.

## Brand Personality

Grim, intimate, consequential. The mood comes from portrait art, restrained color and brief motion. Reading and choosing must remain as simple as Reigns. Characters speak plainly; the interface never substitutes grandiose writing for stakes.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS dashboards, cheerful toy portraits, ornamental metric cards, purple gradients, and mannered historical narration. The original pale document treatment is too gentle for the requested mood.

## Design Principles

- Show one short dilemma and two choices. Everything else must earn its place.
- Put optional world detail behind Menu. Never surround the card with dashboards.
- Put the decision and its human cost at the center.
- Make faction pressure legible before a choice and its effect clear afterward.
- Give arrivals, decisions, and endings distinct motion.
- Preserve setting adaptation and the existing game rules.
- Spend assets and animation on atmosphere without adding model calls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, native dialogs, 44-pixel touch targets, reduced-motion alternatives, and text/icon indicators alongside color. No flashing, forced audio, or motion needed to understand a choice.
